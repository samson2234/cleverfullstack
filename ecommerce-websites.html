The challenge

What your website has to do

Brand-led e-commerce lives and dies on the product page. Shoppers cannot touch the product, so the page has to do it for them: great photos, honest sizing, materials, delivery and returns, all without slowing the page down. Then the checkout has to get out of the way.

We build stores that carry your brand and still load fast on a phone, with product pages that answer the questions shoppers ask and a checkout with no surprises. Whether you are on Shopify or WooCommerce, the store is set up to measure what sells.

Shopify or WooCommerce for a brand store

Shopify is the simpler choice for most growing brands: hosting, security and checkout are handled for you. WooCommerce suits brands that publish a lot of content and want full control without a monthly platform fee. Our Shopify vs WooCommerce guide compares them in detail.

Product pages that answer the questions shoppers ask

Returns are expensive, and most of them start with a question the product page did not answer: will it fit, what does it feel like, how big is it really, when will it arrive. We design product pages around those questions, with size guides and fit notes, materials and care, close-up photos, real dimensions and delivery estimates next to the buy button.

Clear answers lift conversion and cut returns at the same time, which is why we treat the product page as the most important page in the store.

Built for the way your customers shop

Most fashion and lifestyle shopping happens on a phone, often from a social media post. That visitor lands on a product page, not your homepage. We make sure that page loads quickly, makes sense on its own, and offers a fast way to pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or Shop Pay where available.

Common questions

E-Commerce Websites for Brands: answered

How much does an e-commerce website cost?+

Our E-Commerce Store package starts at $1,749 for up to 20 products with checkout, payments, shipping and tax. Larger catalogues and custom features are quoted after a free call.

Can you migrate my store from another platform?+

Yes. We move products, customers and orders where possible, and redirect every old URL to keep your rankings.

Can you sell in several currencies?+

Yes. We set up multi-currency, shipping zones and tax for the markets you sell in.

Do you photograph products?+

No, but we advise on the shots your product pages need and optimise the images you provide.

Can you connect my store to marketplaces and social shops?+

Yes. Shopify and WooCommerce both connect to Google Shopping, Meta and Instagram shops, and to marketplaces such as Amazon through apps or plugins, so your products and stock stay in sync from one place.
