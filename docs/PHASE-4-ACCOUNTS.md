# Phase 4: accounts and profiles to create

These profiles are what Google, Bing and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) use to decide whether CleverStack is a real, trusted business. They have to be created by you (they need your login, email or phone verification). Do them in this order; the first two are the most important.

**For every account:** use `hello@cleverstack.dev`, the website `https://cleverstack.dev`, the logo in `docs/brand/` (`cleverstack-logo-dark-800.png` or `-light-800.png`), and the copy at the bottom of this page. Use the same name everywhere: **CleverStack**.

**Send me back:** the public link of each profile you create. I add them to the site's structured data (`sameAs`), the footer icons and `llms.txt`, which is what connects them to the website for Google and AI tools.

---

## 1. Bing Webmaster Tools (5 minutes, no profile to write)

Why: ChatGPT search and Microsoft Copilot use Bing's index. If Bing doesn't know the site well, ChatGPT can't recommend it.

1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters and sign in with the same Google account you use for Search Console.
2. Choose **Import from Google Search Console**, allow access, select `cleverstack.dev`, and import.
3. In Bing, open **Sitemaps** and check `https://cleverstack.dev/sitemap.xml` is listed (add it if not).

Send me: nothing, just tell me it's done.

## 2. Google Business Profile (15 minutes + verification)

Why: the map results and the "CleverStack" knowledge panel on Google.

1. Go to https://business.google.com and click **Manage now**.
2. Business name: **CleverStack**. Category: **Website designer** (add **Software company** and **Internet marketing service** as extra categories).
3. When asked whether customers visit a location: choose **No** (this makes it a *service-area business* and hides the address).
4. Service areas: add **United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Germany** and other countries you want.
5. Contact: website `https://cleverstack.dev`. You can leave the phone empty or add it privately for verification only.
6. Verification: Google usually asks for a short video (showing your workspace and something that proves the business, such as a laptop showing the site and your business email) or a postcard. Your address stays hidden either way.
7. Add the short description (below), the logo, a cover image, and your services with the prices below.

Send me: the profile's share link (Share profile → copy link).

## 3. LinkedIn company page (10 minutes)

1. On LinkedIn: **For Business → Create a Company Page → Company**.
2. Name **CleverStack**, URL `linkedin.com/company/cleverstack-dev` (or similar; `cleverstack` is taken by another business), website, industry **Software Development**, size **2–10**, type **Privately held**.
3. Add the logo, the tagline and the long description below. Add yourself and the team as employees (each person sets CleverStack as their current job).

Send me: the company page link.

## 4. Clutch (30 minutes + client reviews)

Why: AI assistants and Google frequently cite Clutch for "best web development companies" questions. Reviews here count a lot.

1. Go to https://clutch.co, click **Get Listed** (for service providers) and create a free profile.
2. Fill in: services (Web Development, Mobile App Development, E-Commerce Development, Custom Software Development, SEO), the long description, founding year, team size, and **minimum project size** (choose **$1,000+**, or the lowest option they offer).
3. Clutch asks for an **average hourly rate** band. This is a business decision only you can make; pick the band that honestly matches what you charge.
4. Add 2–3 portfolio items (I suggest Bouhan Falligant, ResearchHUB and Lumea Glow; the descriptions are on the case-study pages under `/work/`).
5. **Reviews:** Clutch verifies reviews by contacting your clients. Use Clutch's "request a review" link and send it with the email template below to 3–5 happy clients.

Send me: the profile link, and tell me when reviews are published.
