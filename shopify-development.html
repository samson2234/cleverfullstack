The problem

Why this is worth doing properly

Most Shopify stores do not fail because of the product. They fail because the store is slow on a phone, the product page leaves questions unanswered, and the checkout asks for too much too soon. Every one of those problems costs sales you already paid to attract with ads or content.

We build Shopify stores the way a buyer shops: fast first screen, clear photos and specs, honest delivery and returns information, and a path to checkout with as few steps as Shopify allows. You get a store that is easy to manage, fully yours, and set up to measure what is actually selling.

Who this is for

  • ✓Brands moving from Etsy, Amazon or social selling to their own store
  • ✓Existing Shopify stores that look dated or convert poorly on mobile
  • ✓Businesses moving from WooCommerce, Wix or Squarespace to Shopify
  • ✓Product launches that need a store ready by a fixed date
  • ✓Teams that want a custom theme without an agency-sized bill
What you get

What's included

Payments, shipping and tax

Checkout and cart that convert

Clear delivery and returns information, trust signals near the buy button, and no surprise costs at the last step.

Speed and SEO foundations

Compressed images, lean apps, clean URLs, product structured data and redirects from your old store so you keep your rankings.

Tracking you can trust

Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel and Google Ads conversion tracking connected and tested, so you know which channel actually sells.

Apps: fewer, chosen on purpose

Every Shopify app adds scripts to your store, and too many apps are the most common reason a store is slow. Before we install anything we check whether the theme can do the job natively. When an app is the right answer, we pick one with a good track record and set it up properly.

If you are moving an existing store, we audit the apps you already have and remove the ones you pay for but do not use. That alone often makes a store noticeably faster.

Migrating to Shopify without losing sales or rankings

Products, customers and order history can be moved from WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace and most other platforms. We map every old product and page URL to its new address with 301 redirects, so search engines and old links send people to the right place.

We launch at a quiet time for your business, test real orders end to end, and keep a close eye on the store in the first days after launch.

Common questions

Shopify Development: answered

How much does a Shopify store cost?+

Our E-Commerce Store package starts at $1,749 and covers up to 20 products, cart and checkout, payments, shipping and tax, search and filtering, and order management. Larger catalogues, custom features or a migration from another platform are quoted after a free call. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

How long does it take to build a Shopify store?+

Most stores are delivered in 10 to 18 days once we have your products, images and branding. A migration or a fully custom theme can take longer, and you will get a specific timeline in your proposal.

Do I need a custom theme or can you customise an existing one?+

Many stores do well on a well-chosen Online Store 2.0 theme that we tailor to your brand. A custom theme makes sense when your products need a layout that standard themes cannot do well. We will recommend the cheaper option when it fits.

Can you move my store from WooCommerce or Wix to Shopify?+

Yes. We move products, customers and order history where the platform allows it, and set up 301 redirects from every old URL so you keep your search rankings.

Will I be able to manage the store myself?+

Yes. You own the store and every account, and we give you a short walkthrough so your team can add products, run discounts and edit pages without a developer.

Do you work with stores outside the USA?+

Yes. We set up Shopify Markets, currencies, shipping zones and tax for stores selling in the USA, UK, Canada and across Europe.
