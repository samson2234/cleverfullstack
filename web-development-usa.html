Common Questions

Web development USA — answered

How much does a business website cost in the USA?+

A professional business website in the USA costs between $499 and $3,000 depending on scope. A starter marketing site starts at $499, a full business website with CMS is $999, e-commerce stores start at $1,749, and custom web applications start at $3,000. All pricing is in USD with no hidden fees.

How long does it take to build a website?+

Most business websites are delivered in 5-14 days. Starter sites take 5-7 days, full business websites take 7-14 days, e-commerce stores take 10-18 days, and custom web applications take 14-28 days. You receive a specific timeline before any work begins.

Do you work with small businesses in the USA?+

Yes. We work with startups, small businesses, and growing companies across the USA — from New York and San Francisco to Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, Miami, and everywhere in between. Our pricing is designed to be accessible for businesses at every stage of growth.

What technologies do you use for web development?+

We use React, Next.js, Node.js, Laravel, WordPress, Shopify, and WooCommerce depending on your project needs. We recommend the best stack for your budget, scalability requirements, and long-term maintenance goals — not the trendiest option.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-optimized?+

Absolutely. Every website we build is mobile-first responsive and SEO-optimized from day one. This includes proper meta tags, structured data, fast load times, Core Web Vitals optimization, and a technical foundation that helps you rank on Google.
