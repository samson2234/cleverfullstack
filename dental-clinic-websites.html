The challenge

What your website has to do

Patients choose a clinic the way they choose any service they are nervous about: they look for reassurance. Who will treat me, what will it involve, what will it cost roughly, and how easily can I book? A clinic website that answers those questions clearly earns the booking. One that hides them behind jargon and stock photos sends people back to search.

We build clinic websites around those questions: treatment pages written in plain language, real practitioners with their qualifications, clear next steps and booking that works on a phone. The design is calm and personal, like a good front desk.

What patients look for before they book

Most clinic enquiries start on a phone, often in the evening. People compare two or three clinics quickly. They look at the practitioner, the treatment explanation, reviews, location and whether booking is easy. Price ranges or "from" prices help, even when the final cost depends on a consultation.

We design around that comparison, so your clinic is the easiest one to understand and the easiest one to book.

Advertising rules

Healthcare advertising has rules in every country, including on before-and-after images, claims about results and testimonials. We build pages that put the facts first, and we ask you to confirm medical claims before anything goes live.

Common questions

Websites for Dentists & Clinics: answered

How much does a dental or clinic website cost?+

Our Business Website package is $999 for up to 10 pages with a CMS, blog, advanced SEO and analytics. Clinics with many treatments or several locations are quoted after a free call, with a fixed price before work starts.

Can you connect our booking system?+

Yes. We embed or link the booking system you already use, such as Dentally, Cliniko, Fresha or Calendly, and put the booking button on every page.

Can we update treatment prices and team members ourselves?+

Yes. Treatments, prices and team profiles are managed in the CMS, and we show your team how at handover.

Do you help with local SEO for clinics?+

Yes. We set up local business schema, location pages and Google Business Profile basics so you appear for searches in your area.

Is patient data safe?+

We keep website forms minimal and avoid collecting medical details by email. For clinical records, patients should use your practice management system, which we link to.
