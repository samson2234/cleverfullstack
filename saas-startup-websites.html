The challenge

What your website has to do

A SaaS website has to do a hard job quickly: explain a product people have never seen, to someone who is comparing you with three alternatives in other tabs. If they cannot tell what you do and who it is for in a few seconds, they close the tab.

We build SaaS marketing sites that lead with the problem you solve, show the product working, make pricing clear and make sign-up easy. And because we also build web apps, we can connect the site to your product properly: sign-up, onboarding emails and analytics that follow a visitor from first page to paying customer.

Who this is for

  • ✓Startups preparing a launch or a funding round
  • ✓SaaS companies whose site no longer matches the product
  • ✓Founders who need both the marketing site and the product built
  • ✓B2B products that need pages per use case or industry

One team for the site and the product

Many startups hire one agency for the website and another for the product, and the gap between them shows: different designs, broken sign-up flows and analytics that stop at the login page. We build both, so the experience from first visit to first value is one piece.

Pages that win searches for your category

Buyers searching for software rarely type your brand name at first. They search for the problem, the job to be done, or an alternative to a tool they already know. Use-case pages, integration pages and honest comparison pages meet them there, and each one can be built from a shared template so your team can add more without a developer.

Because these pages are built on Next.js, they load fast and are easy for search engines to crawl, which matters when you are competing with established players.

Ready for your next stage

A launch site needs to look credible and explain one thing well. A site for a Series A company needs pages for each audience, a customer stories section, a careers page and a docs hub. We build the foundation so the site can grow from one to the other without a rebuild.

Common questions

SaaS & Startup Websites: answered

How much does a SaaS website cost?+

A marketing site starts with our Business Website package at $999. Larger sites with docs and many use-case pages are quoted after a free call.

Can you build the product too?+

Yes. See our SaaS MVP development and React and Next.js services. Product builds start at $3,000.

Which CMS do you use for SaaS sites?+

Often Next.js with a headless CMS such as Sanity, so marketers can publish without developers and the site stays fast.

Can you help with positioning and copy?+

Yes. We help sharpen your message and write page copy from your notes and customer conversations.

How long does a SaaS marketing site take?+

A focused launch site with a homepage, pricing, sign-up and a few supporting pages usually takes 2 to 4 weeks once the message is agreed. Larger sites with docs and many use-case pages take longer, and you get a specific timeline in your proposal.
