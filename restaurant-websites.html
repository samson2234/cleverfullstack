The challenge

What your website has to do

Most people decide where to eat on their phone, often while they are already out. They want three things fast: the menu, the location and a way to book or order. A restaurant website with a PDF menu, a slow gallery and a phone number that is hard to find loses those diners to the place down the road.

We build restaurant websites that make those three things effortless, with photography that does the food justice and a site you can update when the menu changes.

Who this is for

  • ✓Independent restaurants, cafés and bars
  • ✓Restaurant groups with several locations
  • ✓Takeaways and delivery kitchens taking direct orders
  • ✓Small hotels, guesthouses and event venues
What you get

Events and private dining

Local SEO

Restaurant schema with opening hours, menu and location, plus a Google Business Profile that matches the site.

Speed with great photos

Food photography that loads fast on mobile data, so the first impression is the food.

Order direct, keep the margin

Delivery apps bring customers, but they take a large commission on every order. A direct ordering option on your own site, promoted to your regulars, keeps more of that margin. We set it up with your ordering provider and make it the easiest path for people who already know you.

Your Google listing and your website work together

For most restaurants, the first thing a hungry searcher sees is the Google Business Profile, not the website. Opening hours, photos, the menu link and the booking button all appear there. When the website and the profile disagree, for example on opening hours, people lose trust and Google loses confidence in both.

We make the website the single source of truth: structured data for your hours, menu and location, and a profile that links to the right pages. When you change your hours for a holiday, there is one place to update.

Several locations

Restaurant groups need one page per location with its own address, hours, menu differences, booking link and photos. Each location page can rank in its own neighbourhood, and guests land straight on the place they are going to.
