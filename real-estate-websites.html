The challenge

What your website has to do

Property buyers and sellers rarely land on an agency website by accident. They arrive with a question: what is for sale in this area, what is my home worth, who can I trust to sell it. The agents who win are the ones whose website answers those questions fastest, on a phone.

We build real estate websites around search and enquiries: listings that load quickly with good photos, filters that make sense, and valuation and viewing requests that reach the right agent immediately. Your area pages show local knowledge the big portals cannot.

Why build your own site when the portals exist

Portals bring traffic, but the lead belongs to the portal first and you compete with every other agent on the same page. Your own website is where people go after they have heard your name, and where seller leads, the most valuable kind, are won. Strong area pages and a fast valuation form give you leads the portals cannot take a cut of.

Area pages that show you know the market

Buyers and sellers want an agent who knows their street, not just their postcode. Area pages with real knowledge, such as schools, transport, the type of homes and how the local market has moved, give people a reason to choose you over a portal listing. They are also what ranks for searches like "estate agent in" or "homes for sale in" an area.

We set up the page structure and design so your team can write these pages in their own words, and add new areas as you grow.

Portals and custom tools

For property managers and developers, a custom portal can replace email and spreadsheets: tenants report issues, landlords see statements, buyers track a sale. We build these as custom web apps with React, Next.js and Node.js.
