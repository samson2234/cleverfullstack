Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

At CleverStack, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information across the USA, UK, and EU.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us, book a call, or engage our services, including your name, email address, phone number, company name, project details, and payment information processed securely via third-party providers.

Automatically Collected Data

When you visit our website, we automatically collect your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time spent on pages, and date and time of access through analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: provide and maintain our services, communicate with you about projects and inquiries, send service-related notices and updates, improve our website and user experience, process payments and invoicing, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These include essential cookies required for core site functionality, analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors use our website via Vercel Analytics, and marketing cookies used only with your explicit consent. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only with: trusted service providers who assist in operating our website and delivering services (including hosting, analytics, and email platforms), payment processors for secure transaction handling, and legal authorities when disclosure is required by applicable law or to protect our rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Project-related data is retained for the duration of the business relationship and up to three years after its conclusion, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized over time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data:

United States — California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the CCPA grants you the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; the right to request deletion of your personal information; the right to opt out of any sale of personal information (note: we do not sell personal data); and the right to non-discrimination for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

European Union — General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the GDPR, including: the right of access to your personal data, the right to rectification of inaccurate data, the right to erasure (the "right to be forgotten"), the right to restrict processing, the right to data portability, and the right to object to processing. Our legal bases for processing include consent, contractual necessity, and legitimate interests.

United Kingdom — UK GDPR

If you are in the United Kingdom, you have equivalent rights under the UK GDPR as outlined above. Any transfers of personal data outside the UK are conducted with appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where required by the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
