Common Questions

Web development in Europe — answered

How much does a business website cost in Europe?+

A professional business website in Europe costs between €449 and €899 for a standard site. E-commerce stores start at €1,599 and custom web applications from €2,799. All pricing is transparent and in EUR with no hidden fees.

How long does it take to build a website?+

Most business websites are delivered in 5–14 days. E-commerce stores typically take 14–21 days, and custom web applications 21–28 days. We provide a clear timeline before starting any project.

Do you work with small businesses in Europe?+

Yes. We work with startups, small businesses, and enterprises across Europe including Germany, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, Italy, and more. Our scalable solutions fit every budget and growth stage.

What technologies do you use for web development?+

We use modern, battle-tested technologies including React, Next.js, Node.js, Tailwind CSS, and cloud hosting on Vercel. Every site is built mobile-first, SEO-optimized, and fast.

Can you build an e-commerce store for the European market?+

Yes. We build e-commerce stores with multi-currency support (EUR, GBP and more), GDPR-compliant checkout flows, Stripe and Mollie integration, and localized experiences for European customers.
