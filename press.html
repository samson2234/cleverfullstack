Founder Bio

Henry George — Founder & Lead Developer

Henry George is the founder and lead developer of CleverStack, a full-stack development studio specializing in building fast, scalable websites and web applications. With over 5 years of hands-on experience and 68+ projects delivered for clients across the USA, UK, and Europe, Henry leads every engagement from strategy through to production.

Before founding CleverStack, Henry built a reputation as an independent full-stack developer on Fiverr, delivering high-quality web products for international clients. He now leads a team of specialists covering frontend, backend, e-commerce, SEO, and DevOps — providing a single accountable partner for the entire build.

Expertise: React, Next.js, Node.js, PHP, Laravel, WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, and API development.
